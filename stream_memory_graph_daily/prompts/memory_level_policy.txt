Memory-level policy

The system stores the same semantic fields at multiple levels. The target level
changes the desired granularity and stability; it does not change the JSON output
contract of the extraction or fusion task.

Apply this policy conservatively:

- Compress representation, not supported meaning. Preserve concrete values,
  dates, negation, conditions, uncertainty, and meaningful temporal changes.
- A permitted discard applies only when the information is not required by a
  must-retain rule and is not the only evidence for a retained fact or context.
- Do not turn repeated mention into proof of permanence.
- Keep raw source identifiers and evidence provenance separate from the semantic
  representation. Provenance identifiers are not semantic content to summarize.
